## Conventions this restructuring must honour

Defer to the repo's CLAUDE.md and the approved design plan it names for the binding rules —
toolchain, output discipline, the require manifest, the commit-grouping traps. This hole is
where a project pins the *iteration* conventions the shipped scaffold cannot know: when a split
is preferred to a new issue, whether an in-flight issue may be restructured at all, how much
change is allowed before the epic goes back through `plan-epic` instead, and who re-reviews the
gate. Override it at `.lain/slots/skill/iterate-epic/conventions.md`.
