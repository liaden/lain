## Conventions this epic plan must honour

Defer to the repo's CLAUDE.md and the approved design plan it names for the binding rules —
toolchain, output discipline, the require manifest, the commit-grouping traps. This hole is
where a project pins the *decomposition* conventions the shipped scaffold cannot know: the
house id style, how large an issue is allowed to get before it must be cut, whether every issue
needs acceptance criteria at plan time, and who reviews the epic_plan gate. Override it at
`.lain/slots/skill/plan-epic/conventions.md`.
